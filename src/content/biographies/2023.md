Vu Nguyen Ha is a research scientist in the SIGCOM group at SnT. His work applies optimization and machine learning to radio resource management in wireless and satellite networks. He joined SnT in April 2021 after postdoctoral appointments in Montréal.

B.Eng. (Hons.), PFIEV, Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology, 2007; Addendum degree, Télécom Bretagne, 2007; Ph.D. (Hons.), INRS-EMT, Université du Québec, 2017
