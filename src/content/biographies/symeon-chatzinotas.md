Symeon Chatzinotas is a full professor, Chief Scientist I and head of SIGCOM at SnT. His research addresses mobile and satellite communication systems, including 5G/6G connectivity and quantum communications. He also holds an adjunct professorship at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology.

M.Eng., Telecommunications, Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, 2003; M.Sc., Electronic Engineering, University of Surrey, 2006; Ph.D., Electronic Engineering, University of Surrey, 2009
